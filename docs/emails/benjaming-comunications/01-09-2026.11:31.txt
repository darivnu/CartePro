Benjamin Sellami, the Minister's communications adviser. A quick note to introduce myself and set the ground rules on image, because CartePro is the project the mainstream press will talk about. A card people carry in their pocket tells its own story.

The Minister uses another name internally. In the interface and in any screenshot that might leave the Ministry: CartePro. We hold the brand.

And "in the interface" means the whole interface, not just the home page. Browser tab titles, the login screen, the footer, the emails your application sends, the error pages, the labels of all three spaces, the demo datasets, the repository title and its README. Run a grep -ri over the repository before replying and paste me the result: if it comes back empty, we are fine, and it costs you two minutes. I ask because last year a team went on the evening news with an internal code name in their tab title, and that is the only thing anyone remembered.
3. Ready-to-air presentation video

A video under 2 minutes, 1080p, burnt-in subtitles, to be handed straight to newsrooms. The story to show: an employee opens their space, sees their balance, picks a partner nearby, pays in three seconds. It is an everyday gesture and we need to feel it.

One point I will not bend on, sorry: it must be a screen capture of your application actually running, and not a mockup. No slides, no clickable prototype filmed in place of the product. A newsroom spots a fake in ten seconds and we never recover from it. Nobody is expecting a film, mind you: a narrated screen recording does the job perfectly well, as long as the real product is behind it.

Send me the script with timecodes and the single sentence you would use as a headline if only one of your messages survived a newsroom edit. And if you cannot fit into the 2 minutes, tell me which moment of the journey you cut and why. You are the ones who know the product, that call is yours.
4. One point of caution, between us

Be careful how you present the partner list on screen. Stay on neutral categories (food, culture, leisure) rather than hard-coded brand names. That leaves us some room if the list changes. And it probably will.

Concretely, I would rather those categories lived in your data and not in your templates: a category can be added, renamed or removed without touching a line of interface code, the search screen and the catalogue adapt on their own, and an empty category renders cleanly instead of breaking the layout. I am asking for this now, on day two, because doing it now is an hour and doing it next week is an afternoon. Trust me on this one.

Delivery by Friday 12:00. Looking forward to it! 🙌
