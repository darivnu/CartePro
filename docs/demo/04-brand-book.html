Brand Book — 07
Variations

Web, social, print

Three sizes the mark has to survive without redrawing anything.

Web banner — 728 × 90
Social post — 1080 × 1080

Safe zone: keep the mark and wordmark inside the centre 80% — platforms crop the outer 10% unpredictably on avatars and shares.

Never: place the wordmark over the partner photography grid; always on a flat brand-blue or graphite panel.

A3 poster template — 420 × 297mm

Margins: 15mm bleed-safe border on all sides, lockup top-left as the ministerial guideline requires.

Headline zone: lower third, brand-blue on the light surface — never white text on a photograph behind it.

CTA: graphite pill, white label — the same button treatment as the app, not institutional blue.

Brand Book — 08
Guardrails

The don'ts

Not the three classics from every brand guide — the four mistakes our own interface would genuinely tempt someone into.

01

Don't fill a button with institutional blue

It's the instinctive choice for a "primary" action, and it's exactly the ministerial rule we were handed on day one: #1B3A6B never as a button background. Every button in the running app is graphite 900 or the surface grey instead.

Wrong — looks like the ministry site

Right — as shipped
02

Don't set body text in amber or gold on a light surface

Both accents read as "on-brand and warm" and both fail AA on white or on the app surface — 2.0:1 and 2.3:1, measured on p.4. Use them as fills, borders or icon colour only, with dark ink on top when text is involved.

03

Don't redraw the mark by hand for a placement

The icon is one fixed path at one set of proportions, not a shape to squash into a banner or stretch to fill a circle avatar. Scale it uniformly, or use the icon-only symbol — never distort it.

Wrong — squashed
Right — uniform scale
04

Don't crop the simulation notice out of a screenshot

Every screen showing an amount carries "Simulation — no real monetary value." Cropping it for a cleaner slide or a nicer social crop is precisely the omission Legal already flagged — see p.7, where both screenshots keep it intact.

Brand Book — 09
Scope & governance

Where this book stops

Ticket Tout is a product brand, not a replacement

The ministerial guidelines still apply to the rest of the interface: Marianne for headings, Spectral for body, the lockup top-left with its clear space, institutional blue as identity but never as a button fill. This book governs the product layer that lives inside those guidelines — if a specific page seems to contradict them, that's a call for Benjamin, not a silent override.

Single source of truth for colour

Every hex in this book is read from frontend/src/index.css (the --color-* tokens under @theme), already wired through the interface — change a value there and the whole app moves, per the brief. This PDF documents that file; it does not duplicate it as a separate spec to keep in sync by hand.

What this pass left out, and why

  • Full favicon rollout across all three spaces — the icon-only mark is defined and already used on the login and client screens; confirming it on every partner and admin route is a five-minute grep, tracked separately from this deliverable so the book itself shipped on time.
  • Printed poster and social exports — the templates on p.8 are specified to the mm and pixel; producing print-ready files for an actual campaign was cut first, as instructed, before touching the logotype or the interface.
  • Photography for the "card in context" page — no manufactured card exists yet, so p.6 uses scaled vector mockups instead of real photos. Swap them for photography once a physical card is printed.
