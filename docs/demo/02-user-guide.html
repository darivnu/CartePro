Step by step: the citizen (client) journey

Follow these steps in order. Tap exactly what's described below.

1Log in

Tap the Email field, type the account's email. Tap Password, type the password. Tap the black LOG IN button.

The screen opens on this card automatically — nothing to do before this.

2Home screen

After logging in, this is the home screen. The balance is shown at the top (here, 42,50 €), always labelled as simulated money. Two buttons below: Generate QR code and Browse partners.

3Generate a payment QR code

Tap GENERATE QR CODE.

A real, scannable QR code appears immediately with a countdown. Tap the × in the top corner (or tap outside the card) to close it.

4Browse partners

From the home screen, tap BROWSE PARTNERS.

Shows every approved shop — currently 6: Test Partner, Boulangerie Martin, Pharmacie Centrale, Garage Dupont, Librairie Voltaire, Salon Elegance.

5Search a partner

Tap the Search partners field and type a business name, e.g. "Boulangerie".

The list filters live as you type — no need to press enter. Safe search words: Boulangerie, Pharmacie, Garage, Librairie, Salon, Test.

6Open a partner's details

Tap any result in the list.

Shows the business's category, region, and address. Tap BACK (top right) to return to the list.
