TICKET TOUT

Prototype status, in plain language

Where the app stands

What the app actually does today — not what's planned or half-written in the code.

⏱ under 2 minutes to read

Works right now

  • ✓Logging in as a citizen (client) account — reliable, fast.
  • ✓Seeing your balance on the home screen, clearly labelled "Simulation — no real monetary value" everywhere money appears.
  • ✓Generating a payment QR code — a real, scannable code appears instantly with a live countdown. It's single-use and stays valid for about half an hour.
  • ✓Browsing partner businesses — a real list of 6 approved shops (a bakery, a pharmacy, a garage, a bookshop, a hairdresser, plus one generic test entry), each with category and region.
  • ✓Searching and filtering that list by name or category — updates live as you type.
  • ✓Opening a business's detail page — name, category, region, address.

Not built / not working yet

  • ✕Purchase history on the home screen isn't available yet — that part of the server hasn't been finished.
  • ✕The shop (partner) side exists as a login only — after signing in it's a blank page with just a logout button. No screen yet to scan or accept a citizen's QR code.
  • ✕The oversight (admin) side is the same — login works, but the screen behind it is blank. No screen yet to review or approve new shop applications.
  • ✕Signing up for a new account isn't available in the app at all. Every account (citizen, shop, or admin) has to be created ahead of time by the dev team.
  • ✕Adding funds to a balance isn't available anywhere yet — balances are fixed at whatever they were set to when the account was created.

Next week: complete

  • ✓Full transaction flow — a citizen's QR code scanned and validated by a shop, end to end.
  • ✓The shop (partner) space — validating a payment, a financial dashboard, browsing the partner catalog.
  • ✓The oversight (admin) space — approving new shop applications, a national dashboard, and "Minister's Pick" to highlight a partner on the citizen home screen.
  • ✓Purchase history on the citizen home screen.
  • ✓Technical documentation and project retrospective.

Good to know

  • •The rebrand from "CartePro" to "Ticket Tout" is complete — the running app shows the new name and logo everywhere, no old branding left.
