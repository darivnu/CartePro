TICKET TOUT

Plan B — if something looks wrong on screen

What to say, exactly what to tap. No need to understand why it happens.

1

Login shows a red "Unauthorized" message

Means the email or password was mistyped — easy to do live on camera with a tablet keyboard.

Say: "Let me double-check the login details."
Tap: clear both fields and retype slowly from the printed credentials card — don't rely on memory.
2

Screen just says "Partner area" or "Admin area" with nothing else

Means the wrong account logged in — those two are internal, unbuilt pages, not part of the demo.

Say: "That's the back-office view for shop and city staff — let's go back to the citizen view."
Tap: LOG OUT (top right), then log back in with the citizen account.
3

Partner search says "No partners found."

Only 6 businesses exist in the catalog, so an unplanned or misspelled search can come up empty.

Say: "Let's try one of our partners" — while switching the search term.
Tap: clear the search field completely (select all, delete) to instantly show all 6 again. Safe words: Boulangerie, Pharmacie, Garage, Librairie, Salon.
4

Blank grey screen for a few seconds after opening the app

Normal first-load delay — worse on a cold tablet browser or slow wifi.

Say: "One second, just loading."
Tap: nothing — wait 5–10 seconds. Do not refresh or tap LOG IN twice; that restarts the wait.
